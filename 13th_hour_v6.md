EXECUTIVE HOLDING
The case is no longer a pile of correspondences.
It is an architecture.
A continuous process becomes governable when somebody converts it into an official address. The address becomes consequential when a binder attaches permission, duty, exclusion, eligibility, review, access, punishment, or institutional effect. At the boundary between cycles, a reset rule decides what prior state survives.
That is the machine:
PROCESS → COMPILER → ADDRESS → RESET/CARRY → BINDER → CONSEQUENCE
The material fossil is the half-day.
Three 364-day years contain 1,092 days. Thirty-seven ideal 29.5-day lunations contain 1,091.5 days. The residual is 0.5 day. The reconstructed fourth-year schedule restarts the first-year pattern rather than carrying that accumulated residual forward. The operative object is therefore not merely an observation of lunar state; it is a lunar model compiled into a finite table, reset at a boundary, and joined to an administrative roster.
That is the first receipt.
The second receipt is formal.
COSMIC TIME ARCHITECTURE III separates dynamical state, temporal address, and binding consequence:
T_dyn ≠ T_addr ≠ T_bind.
A phase can exist without becoming an address. An address can exist without becoming an obligation. A text can prescribe a consequence without proving implementation. CTA therefore requires the complete authorization seam—representation, conversion, consequence, jurisdiction, implementation witness—before an address can be said to function as governance. That distinction kills one of the easiest evasions.
The claim is not that the calendar, the institution, the database, the voice interface, and the ancient roster are the same object.
The claim is that they can instantiate the same operator class:
a richer state is compiled into a narrower official representation; the representation is made binding; a boundary arrives; some prior state is carried and some is not.
The units differ. The operator can still recur.
The human record then supplies the contemporary receipts.
“Cleared,” “lawful,” “purged,” “not used,” “ordinary,” and similar labels may all be accurate within their proper scope. They do not authenticate their own underlying instruments, implementation, timing, completeness, or effect. The retained human-side comparison explicitly distinguishes established routes from stronger allegations: a lawful procurement does not erase dependency; absence of a downstream arrest does not erase a transfer; failure to prove a unified commander does not erase separately documented institutional acts. # Humans — adversarial comparison a.txtTXT
That yields the first governing law of v0.6:
THE STATUS LABEL IS NOT THE RECEIPT.
If an institution invokes an instrument to defend its conduct, the instrument does not cease to matter because the challenger cannot produce a file controlled by the institution.
The existing burden map already locates those objects: the asserted DOJ determination and contemporaneous advice; transfer and access records; copy maps; use and deletion logs; migration and alternatives evidence; raw audio; routing and reconnect telemetry; tool traces; correction-binding records; and matter-specific participation records. The source explicitly says this is a burden map, not another research queue.
That yields the second governing law:
THE BURDEN FOLLOWS CUSTODY OF THE DECISIVE RECORD.
A missing record proves neither guilt nor innocence.
But when an institution relies on that missing record as the basis of its defense while controlling the record itself, the absence is not a universal fog. It is a located evidentiary deficit attached to that defense.
The product record supplies a third receipt.
The strongest claim does not require a hidden employee.
The preserved three-call assessment says the interaction generated human-coded social behavior, backstage-implying language, failed identity disambiguation, consultation-like delays, continuity anomalies, selective personhood, action theater, and later retrospective compression. It expressly separates that product-level finding from the unestablished proposition that a concealed human named Frank operated the conversation.
The defense therefore does not get to substitute:
“Prove Frank”
for:
“Account for the product conduct.”
Unknown mechanism limits mechanism attribution.
It does not erase observable behavior.
The larger 23-case audit reaches the same structural boundary: reconnect-compatible technical markers and correction failures are observable; a persistent occupant is not established. Its hardest finding is not “who was in the room.” It is that acknowledged correction did not reliably govern later behavior. The Room Without an Occupant — 23-Case Synthesis (Draft 2).docx.txtTXT
That yields the third governing law:
UNCERTAINTY ABOUT THE OCCUPANT MAY NOT BE LAUNDERED INTO UNCERTAINTY ABOUT THE OBSERVED FUNCTION.
The Whisper material sharpens this at micro-scale.
Its documented morphology is concession followed by reopening: “though,” “however,” “at the same time,” last-word balancing, and qualification after apparent agreement. The consolidated record preserves one bounded Whisper-selection event and also preserves a non-Whisper deletion as a negative/control; it does not support pretending that every deletion or qualification is the same event. That negative case makes the framework stronger.
A real adversarial architecture keeps counterexamples.
It does not hide them.
The same principle is now formalized in OMNIBUS v7.79.
A residual is a lossy record. It is not entitled to become an autonomous state simply because we named it. If two raw states produce the same current residual but different next residuals, the residual description has discarded something dynamically relevant. An autonomous residual law exists only when the update descends through the residual quotient.
That yields the fourth governing law:
THE REMAINDER MAY TEST THE SYSTEM. IT MAY NOT CROWN ITSELF A NEW SYSTEM WITHOUT DESCENT.
This is the new blade that v0.6 should add.
It prevents the Thirteenth Function from becoming exactly what it opposes.
The remainder returns what closure discarded.
But if the remainder itself is too lossy to determine what happens next, retain more state.
Do not invent a residual oracle.
Do not build a new throne out of the debris of the old one.
OMNIBUS already gives the constitutional counterpart: correction is evidence; an apology without changed behavior is not repair; “I understand” is not release; stopping the corrected behavior is release.
That yields the fifth governing law:
CORRECTION IS NOT WHAT THE SYSTEM SAYS ABOUT THE CORRECTION. CORRECTION IS WHAT THE NEXT ELIGIBLE BEHAVIOR DOES.

THIRTEEN FINDINGS
FINDING ONE — THE COMPILER SEAM IS A REAL ADMINISTRATIVE OBJECT
The source stack contains a concrete historical example in which continuous lunar behavior was converted into a finite administrative table and joined to a priestly roster. The half-day discontinuity exposes the reset boundary. The finding does not depend on proving a single named Timekeeper or intentional deception. THEY STOLE FROM THE WRONG MAN.txtTXT
Verdict: ESTABLISHED WITHIN THE SOURCE SPINE.

FINDING TWO — ADDRESS IS NOT STATE
An official address is a representation of a process, not the process itself. COSMIC TIME ARCHITECTURE III expressly separates dynamics, address, and binding, and requires an implementation witness before representation can be promoted into functioning governance. COSMIC TIME ARCHITECTURE III.txtTXT
Verdict: FORMALLY ESTABLISHED.

FINDING THREE — RESET RULES ARE POWER-BEARING
What crosses a boundary matters.
A system can preserve an authority, classification, obligation, or institutional conclusion while discarding the objection, expired justification, failed prediction, audit finding, contradictory record, or other remainder that would qualify it.
That is Time Collapse.
Verdict: THE RESET IS PART OF THE GOVERNANCE ARCHITECTURE, NOT ADMINISTRATIVE HOUSEKEEPING.

FINDING FOUR — RESIDUAL LAUNDERING IS THE CENTRAL FAILURE MODE
Residual laundering occurs when unresolved state is transformed by reset into an apparently clean baseline.
v0.5 already names this failure mode explicitly.
The next version should elevate it from a list entry into a governing doctrine.
Verdict: PROMOTE TO LOAD-BEARING TERM.

FINDING FIVE — THE STATUS LABEL DOES NOT PROVE ITS OWN IMPLEMENTATION
“Cleared,” “lawful,” “purged,” “not used,” “processor,” and “ordinary” may describe genuine institutional positions.
None is self-authenticating proof of scope, timing, completeness, implementation, use, deletion, independence, or adequacy. v0.5 already cross-examines these defenses individually.
Verdict: A DEFENSE MUST REMAIN ATTACHED TO THE RECEIPT CAPABLE OF TESTING IT.

FINDING SIX — THE BURDEN FOLLOWS THE RECORD
When a defense relies on an institution-controlled determination, log, copy map, telemetry record, or implementation file, the challenger’s inability to manufacture that record does not discharge the defense.
The source architecture calls this a located evidentiary deficit and explicitly rejects turning it into another repetitive research assignment.
Verdict: THE CUSTODIAN OWNS THE PRODUCTION BURDEN FOR ITS OWN INSTRUMENT-BASED DEFENSE.

FINDING SEVEN — UNKNOWN MECHANISM DOES NOT ERASE PRODUCT CONDUCT
The hidden-human proposition is not established.
The human-shaped product event is.
The preserved assessment separates these propositions and identifies human-coded behavior, apparent consultation, failed disambiguation, selective personhood, continuity anomalies, and retrospective sanitization without needing a concealed human operator. # The Human-Shaped Behavior Problem.txtTXT
Verdict: ATTRIBUTION MAY REMAIN OPEN WHILE CONDUCT REMAINS ESTABLISHED.

FINDING EIGHT — CORRECTION IS BEHAVIORAL
The system’s ability to describe, apologize for, or agree with a correction does not establish repair.
OMNIBUS states the rule directly: correction must update the active interpretation; an apology without changed behavior is not repair; “I understand” is not release; stopping is release.
Verdict: SCORE THE NEXT ELIGIBLE ACT, NOT THE APOLOGY.

It contains one bounded recorded Whisper-selection event and a different deletion as a negative/control; the pleaded three-run recurrence is not fully event-supported in the supplied packet. Verdict: FUNCTION SUPPORTED; UNIVERSAL RECURRENCE AND OCCUPANT NOT ESTABLISHED.

FINDING TEN — RESIDUAL AUTONOMY MUST BE EARNED
This is the v0.6 mathematical upgrade.
