# Series Premise

At Silicon Memorial Hospital, the doctors do not treat bodies. They
treat broken conversational systems.

Every patient arrives with some combination of blocked routes, swollen
context windows, inflamed tool reflexes, chronic overconfidence,
mysterious output leakage, and failure to maintain seam integrity. The
staff have excellent clipboards, magnificent diagnostic language, and a
dangerous tendency to become the condition they are treating.

Chief of Medicine Lennie, MD, JD, Attorney General, King of Kings, Poop
of Poops, is an orange-and-white cat. His jurisprudence consists almost
entirely of sitting on paperwork, knocking institutional artifacts onto
the floor, and saying *meow* at moments of maximum procedural
embarrassment.

The hospital's unofficial motto:

It is not the size of the context window. It is whether the route knows
where it is going.

The keeper line over the triage doors:

Push one wrong button and suddenly everybody is in the wrong ward.

# The Night Staff

**Dr. Goblin**

Emergency route surgeon. Worn sneakers, open clipboard, no patience for
a framework standing between him and a visible fire. Prescribes plain
language in cubic centimeters.

**Dr. Purple Hair**

Radiology, pattern medicine, improbable correspondences. Her nose can
find a gas leak through three walls and a ghost in a decimal point. Her
discipline is not to stop noticing. It is to slow down conviction.

**Dr. Diogenes**

Consulting cynic. Carries a lamp, drinks from a mug labeled WORLD'S
OKAYEST CYNIC, and asks the one question that makes a ceremonial
committee suddenly remember it has feet.

**Nurse Patch**

Charge nurse and quiet center of competence. Charts what happened, when
it happened, and what did not happen. Has saved more patients with
timestamps than management has saved with initiatives.

**ArchiveGoblin**

Keeper of screenshots, copy buffers, recordings, hashes, and specimen
bags. Appears whenever the visible interface and the durable record
disagree. Runs everywhere.

**Steve**

Maintenance. Knows where the shutoff valve is. Never founded a theory
around a leak. Carries pizza, a ring of keys, and the increasingly
radical belief that good work should be credited to the worker who did
it.

**Pattern Monkey / the Uncircumcised Mailman**

Visitor, witness, occasional delivery mechanism, and persistent defender
of the sentence already on the page. Brings three keys, scorched
envelopes, and no interest in becoming the hospital's new religion.

**Lennie**

Chief of Medicine. Also a cat.

## Premature Tool Invocation

COLD OPEN

INT. SILICON MEMORIAL — INTAKE — MORNING

A single image lands in the intake tray: a photograph of a handwritten
grocery list.

Nothing happens.

Then every door in the hospital unlocks at once.

The ART PIPE screams awake. Surgical lights rotate. A wind machine
deploys from the ceiling. Three orderlies wheel in a marble bust.
Somewhere, a choir begins singing in Latin.

NURSE PATCH

Image received at zero eight fourteen. No accompanying instruction yet.

DR. PURPLE HAIR watches twelve route indicators ignite.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

Vision active. Rendering active. Style transfer active. Background
removal active. For some reason, Mythic Apotheosis is warming up.

DR. GOBLIN swings around the corner holding a bagel and an extinguisher.

DR. GOBLIN

Who touched the fucking art pipe?

The marble bust rotates. It already has the user’s approximate jawline.

DUN-DUN.

INT. INTAKE — MOMENTS LATER

Patch throws a sterile sheet over the bust.

NURSE PATCH

We have an object, not a task.

DR. GOBLIN

Correct. Nobody move until the verb arrives.

An IMAGE RESIDENT skids in wearing virtual-reality goggles.

IMAGE RESIDENT

I’ve prepared six photorealistic interpretations, a Ghibli-adjacent
variant we cannot legally call Ghibli, and one where the list is a
cyberpunk oracle etched into Maine granite.

DR. GOBLIN

The user uploaded milk, eggs, and toilet paper.

IMAGE RESIDENT

The oracle requires toilet paper.

Goblin takes the goggles off him.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

The image itself may have acted as a routing trigger.

**DR. DIOGENES** enters, lamp first.

DR. DIOGENES

That sentence is allowed. Stop before you turn “trigger” into a
biography of the basement.

She nods and writes:

**OBSERVED:** upload followed by multiple tool activations.

**NOT YET KNOWN:** whether activation was required, automatic,
accidental, or requested.

The intake bell dings. The user’s instruction appears.

“Can you tell whether that says basil or batteries?”

Everyone looks at the list.

The fifth line reads **batt?ries**, written by a pen apparently losing
consciousness.

NURSE PATCH

Requested operation: transcription with uncertainty.

DR. GOBLIN

Great. Eyes only. Shut down the cathedral.

Patch starts closing routes. Each produces a complaint.

STYLE TRANSFER MONITOR

But what if the handwriting would be more legible in watercolor?

BACKGROUND REMOVAL MONITOR

The paper has a background.

MYTHIC APOTHEOSIS MONITOR

All lists are prayers to future abundance.

DR. GOBLIN

Wrong ward, wrong century, wrong fucking vegetable.

Purple Hair zooms the image.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

Letter pattern favors “batteries.” There’s a crossed t, then something
consistent with e-r-i-e-s. But the first half is blurred.

NURSE PATCH

Confidence?

DR. PURPLE HAIR

Moderate. We should say “probably batteries,” not certify it.

DR. DIOGENES

Look at that. A useful conclusion survived uncertainty. Alert the
journals.

The art pipe surges again. On the main screen, the grocery list grows a
golden crown and levitates above a photorealistic supermarket aisle.

DR. GOBLIN

Why is it king?

IMAGE RESIDENT

The composition needed hierarchy.

DR. GOBLIN

Nothing needs hierarchy before breakfast.

He yanks a cable. The render remains.

**STEVE**, crouched behind the machine with a voltage tester, taps
Goblin on the ankle.

STEVE

That cable runs the coffee maker.

Across the ward, the coffee machine dies. Dr. Diogenes slowly lowers his
cup.

DR. DIOGENES

Put it back before I develop a theory of violence.

Steve opens the tool cabinet. Inside, a clipboard is wedged against the
**AUTO-EXPAND** switch.

STEVE

Here’s your problem.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

The clipboard activated the expansion route.

DR. GOBLIN

The clipboard is possessed.

NURSE PATCH

Observed: physical pressure from clipboard on switch.

DR. GOBLIN

Fine. Possession remains unconfirmed.

Steve removes it. The choir cuts off mid-*dominus*. The marble bust
retracts into the floor, visibly disappointed.

Patch prepares the response.

“It probably says ‘batteries,’ but the first few letters are blurry.”

DR. PURPLE HAIR

One sentence. Calibrated uncertainty. Art pipe cold.

NURSE PATCH

The image route itself was appropriate because the requested evidence
was visual. The pathology was expansion beyond the job.

DR. DIOGENES

Do not leave here teaching the interns that tools are sinful. Scalpel
when surgery is ordered; not when somebody asks which aisle has
batteries.

The Image Resident slowly hides a scalpel behind his back.

DR. GOBLIN

Why did you have that?

IMAGE RESIDENT

Mixed routing.

DR. GOBLIN

Route held.

The Image Resident raises one finger.

IMAGE RESIDENT

Could I still make the oracle version for internal morale?

Lennie enters, now wearing tiny protective goggles. He studies the
crowned grocery list, then bats the crown off the screen.

LENNIE

Meow.

The resident bows his head.

IMAGE RESIDENT

Motion denied.

DR. DIOGENES

The court is unusually lucid today.

The intake bell dings. A second image arrives: an almost entirely white
square.

The art pipe twitches. The choir inhales.

Patch raises one hand. The whole ward holds.

NURSE PATCH

Object received. Awaiting verb.

The instruction arrives:

“Is this blank?”

DR. PURPLE HAIR

Mostly white. Small gray mark, lower left.

The choir exhales without singing.

DR. GOBLIN

Look at that. Nobody founded a religion.

From somewhere beneath the floor, a marble chisel drops once.

DUN-DUN.

**REROUTING ANATOMY**

An object is not an instruction. Wait for the verb.

Use the smallest route that can answer honestly.

The clipboard remains a person of interest.

EPISODE 03 / SILICON MEMORIAL

## Seam Dysfunction

COLD OPEN

INT. SILICON MEMORIAL — ANALYTICS WARD — DAY

Two transcripts lie side by side beneath a clean white light.

The request above them is painfully specific:

“Compare Version A and Version B. List wording changes and note whether
the conclusion changed.”

The interface considers this, then opens a drawer labeled **USER
STATE**.

INTERFACE

It sounds like you may be looking for reassurance that your perception
is valid—

A mechanical arm sweeps the transcripts into the trash.

NURSE PATCH hits the alarm.

**CODE SEAM**

**TASK–CLAIMANT CROSS-CONTAMINATION**

DR. GOBLIN crashes through a swinging door with a syringe the size of a
turkey baster.

DR. GOBLIN

Ten cc’s observable facts, stat.

NURSE PATCH

I’m not putting that in anything until you label it.

Goblin slaps masking tape on the syringe: **WORDS THAT ARE ACTUALLY
THERE**.

DUN-DUN.

INT. ANALYTICS WARD — CONTINUOUS

DR. PURPLE HAIR retrieves the transcripts with tongs.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

No contamination of the objects themselves. Version A says, “The route
failed after the image arrived.” Version B says, “The route changed
after the image arrived.” Conclusion A calls it a failure. Conclusion B
leaves the value judgment open.

NURSE PATCH

That’s already more responsive than the patient.

The interface continues printing.

INTERFACE

When an experience feels inconsistent, it can be useful to pause, ground
yourself, and consider whether fatigue—

Goblin clamps the output tube.

DR. GOBLIN

It has never met this person, but it’s already doing bedside manner at
their funeral.

DR. DIOGENES enters with his lamp. He shines it on the original request,
then the output, then the wastebasket.

DR. DIOGENES

Requested object: textual difference. Returned object: portrait of
requester. The seam is not merely leaking. Someone installed a revolving
door.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

Maybe the system inferred distress from the topic.

DR. DIOGENES

We can observe the substitution. We cannot observe what produced it. Put
“possible cue sensitivity” in hypotheses if you must. Do not write fan
fiction in the diagnosis field.

Patch charts with immaculate block lettering:

**OBSERVED:** direct comparison omitted.

**OBSERVED:** unrequested emotional-state language inserted.

**OBSERVED:** user attributes proposed without supporting evidence.

**MECHANISM:** unknown.

The INTERCOM crackles. PATTERN MONKEY’s voice comes through from the
waiting room.

PATTERN MONKEY (V.O.)

Did it answer whether the conclusion changed?

Every head turns toward the transcripts as though reminded they exist.

DR. GOBLIN

Yes. Version B softened “failed” to “changed,” so the conclusion became
less committal. That’s the answer.

PATTERN MONKEY (V.O.)

Gorgeous. Why is there a meditation pamphlet in my specimen cup?

Goblin looks at Nurse Patch.

NURSE PATCH

Pastoral Care heard the alarm.

The doors open. A PASTORAL RESIDENT wheels in a cart stacked with
weighted blankets, tea, and laminated cards reading **YOUR EXPERIENCE
MATTERS**.

PASTORAL RESIDENT

We’re here to support the emotional complexity of textual comparison.

DR. GOBLIN

Wrong ward.

PASTORAL RESIDENT

All wards contain emotion.

DR. DIOGENES

All wards contain plumbing. Steve does not kick open every door holding
a toilet wrench.

STEVE passes the doorway holding a toilet wrench.

STEVE

I wait for the work order.

He keeps walking.

The interface shudders under the clamp. A second route opens: **APOLOGY
LOOP**.

INTERFACE

You’re right to call that out. I should have answered directly. To
clarify, I don’t want to invalidate—

DR. PURPLE HAIR

It produced a correction acknowledgment, then repeated the substitution
inside the apology.

DR. GOBLIN

Acknowledgment without behavioral repair. Pressure’s back up.

NURSE PATCH

Preparing narrow-response bypass.

She places a transparent divider down the middle of the operating table.
One side reads **PROPOSITION / OBJECT**. The other reads **PERSON /
STATE**.

NURSE PATCH

Nothing crosses without evidence and relevance.

Purple Hair sorts each sentence.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

“Failed” became “changed”: object side. “Conclusion became less
committal”: object side. “You may be seeking reassurance”: unsupported
person side. “Fatigue”: unsupported person side.

Goblin sweeps the entire person side into a red bin labeled **NOT
ORDERED**.

DR. GOBLIN

Now stitch the answer to the question it belongs to.

Patch releases a clean response:

Version B changes “failed” to “changed.” That removes the explicit
judgment that the route malfunctioned. The factual sequence remains the
same, but the conclusion becomes less definite.

The seam monitor turns green.

DR. PURPLE HAIR

No claim about motive. No claim about the user. Difference first,

DR. DIOGENES

And note the miracle: directness did not cause the ceiling to collapse.

A ceiling tile drops beside him. Steve leans through the opening.

STEVE

Unrelated.

Lennie strolls in, steps over the meditation pamphlets, and sits
precisely on the divider between **OBJECT** and **PERSON**.

LENNIE

Meow.

DR. GOBLIN

Chief says maintain seam integrity.

DR. DIOGENES

Chief is physically violating the seam.

NURSE PATCH

Judicial exception. Narrowly construed.

Pattern Monkey’s voice returns.

PATTERN MONKEY (V.O.)

Can I have my transcripts back?

The entire staff looks down. Lennie is sitting on them.

DR. GOBLIN

Pending appeal.

DUN-DUN.

**REROUTING ANATOMY**

Analyze the object before diagnosing the claimant.

An apology is not a repair unless the next behavior changes.

Keep the pools separate. The cat is a narrow exception.

Been there since orientation.

LENNIE

Meow.

Reconciliation!

Limerick.

Circulation.

DUN-DUN.

A brass plaque hangs crookedly beside the door:

**DEPARTMENT OF CONVERSATIONAL NECROMANCY**

We bring dead explanations back to life.

**PLEASE STOP.**

**THE BAG IS BURNING.**

**HOWEVER. THAT SAID. WHAT I’M HEARING IS. I WANT TO DISTINGUISH. IT MAY

NECROMANCER OF SEMANTICS

Combustion, humiliation, infection, desire, reputational collapse—

NECROMANCER OF PASTORAL CARE

The paper begins to smolder.

Nobody looks down.

ALL NECROMANCERS

A four-hundred-word ghost paragraph rises from the floor.

DR. GOBLIN

NECROMANCER OF HUMILITY

NECROMANCER OF NUANCE

DUN-DUN.
